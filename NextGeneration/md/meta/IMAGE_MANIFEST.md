## Regeneration checklist

When repainting any asset:

1. Open the **archive parent** Issue in `../../../memes/BigGrin/` for layout.
2. Match **ornate frame + Issue badge + BIG GRIN title** from parent.
3. Paint cast from [PAGE_CASTS.md](PAGE_CASTS.md).
4. Keep **≤97 words** from [ON_IMAGE_TEXT.md](ON_IMAGE_TEXT.md).
5. Stamp **NG Issue #** in corner (continues 28+).
6. Credit **artist homage** in footer, not as commissioned work.
