# Artist Style Credits — BIG GRIN Comic Homages

Every image in `assets/` is an **original AMOURANTHRTX advertisement** rendered as a *BIG GRIN* comic-book cover — ornate borders, roses, eyes, cracked rainbow skies, Issue numbers, and the occasional xenomorph gag.

**Canonical archive:** [`../../../memes/BigGrin/`](../../../memes/BigGrin/) (Issues 1–27). Next Generation continues at **Issue 28**. Style bible: [BIG-GRIN-COMICS.md](../info/BIG-GRIN-COMICS.md).
