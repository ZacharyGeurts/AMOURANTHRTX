## How to read a legend

1. **The Cover** — ≤97 words on paint. OCR lies; [ON_IMAGE_TEXT.md](ON_IMAGE_TEXT.md) wins.
2. **The Legend** — who we remember and why this issue exists.
3. **The Interview** — what the field actually does (wiki pages carry the long form).
4. **The Tiers** — Beginner runs it · Intermediate maps it · Expert cites the header.
